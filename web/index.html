Namoona — yeh example hai, live analysis nahi
    Poori analysis
    Bot ka tareeqa: entry kaise dhoondta hai
    1. Trend pehle (trend is your friend). 1D/4H/1H par higher highs/lows ya lower highs/lows. Main trade hamesha isi direction mein; ulti trade ho to bot "counter-trend" ki warning deta hai.
    2. Structure. BOS (body close se purana high/low toota, trend jaari) aur CHoCH (pehla ulta break, trend badalne ka ishara).
    3. Liquidity. Equal highs/lows, Asia high/low, pichle din ka high/low. Bari parties wahan ke stop losses lene ke baad hi chalti hain, is liye bot dekhta hai kaunsi liquidity le li gayi aur kaunsi agla target hai.
    4. Key level. Strong support/resistance, supply/demand zone, Order Block ya FVG. Jis level par kai timeframes milte hain wahi chuna jata hai.
    5. Premium/Discount. Range ka beech (0.5). Buy sasti jagah (neeche aadhe) se, sell mehngi jagah (upar aadhe) se.
    6. Breakout real hai ya fake? Real: candle ki body level ke bahar close, bari momentum candle, peeche FVG, retest par level hold. Fake: sirf wick bahar, candle wapas andar close, foran ulta move, aksar Asia session mein.
    7. Entry (1m/5m). Teen tareeqe: retest (level toota, wapas aaya, hold kiya), breakout (momentum ke baad pehla pullback), liquidity sweep reversal (fake breakout ke baad ulti taraf). Entry tabhi jab chote timeframe par CHoCH ya rejection mile.
    8. SL aur TP. SL us wick/zone ke peeche jahan idea ghalat sabit ho. TP1 pehli liquidity par, TP2 agli bari liquidity par. Kam az kam 1:3 ki koshish.
    9. Trade management. Har trade ke saath waqt diya jata hai. TP1 par aadha profit aur SL entry par, baqi trail. Waqt guzar jaye aur TP1 na aaye to trade band, taa ke achi trade zyada der hold karne se SL na lage.

    Grade A+/A: zyada tar cheezein match. B: theek. C: kamzor, chhota size ya sirf confirmation ke baad.

    Levels screenshot se parhe jaate hain aur galat ho sakte hain; har entry se pehle live chart par confirm karein aur risk 1–2% se zyada na rakhein. Yeh analysis mein madad ka tool hai, financial advice nahi.
